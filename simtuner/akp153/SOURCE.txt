The Windows CI workflow checks out this commit, builds opendeck-akp153.exe, and copies it to
st.lynx.plugins.opendeck-akp153.sdPlugin/opendeck-akp153-win.exe before the NSIS build.
The binaries are not stored in this repository.

Display name for AKP153, AKP153E (0300:1010), and AKP153E rev. 2 (0300:3010) is "Sim Deck".
Device ids are unchanged:
- rev. 1 AKP153E: 99-355499441494-153E
- rev. 2 AKP153E: 99-<serial>
- AKP153: 99-355499441494-153
